Who we are

mySMB.com is an IT services company focused on bringing practical, AI-driven change to small and medium businesses at scale.

Workspace gives your business the productivity tools and support to run day-to-day operations from one place, while mySMB.com Community gives you a place to connect, learn and discover trusted solutions.

Our learning platform, Academy, offers self-paced community learning programs and adoption pathways, backed by on-demand and live webinar series, so building AI confidence fits around your working week rather than the other way around.

We also provide ready-to-use business and legal templates, practical how-to guides, and a carefully vetted marketplace of third-party solutions, so you're never starting from a blank page.

For businesses with more advanced needs, we offer optional add-ons, including pre-built AI agents, a custom AI Studio service, and access to practical, plain-English legal advice through mySMB Legal.

We're purpose-built to support the underserved SMB segment: sole traders, small and medium businesses, and not-for-profit organisations.
