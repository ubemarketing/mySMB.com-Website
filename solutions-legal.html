A law firm designed to make legal support accessible for small and medium businesses.

mySMB Legal Pty Ltd is an incorporated legal practice designed specifically for small and medium businesses in Australia. We offer practical, commercial, business-aligned legal support in plain English, delivered via our monthly subscriptions or fixed fee packages. Our mission is simple: to embed practical legal support into your team on an ongoing basis, so you can focus on growth with confidence that your business is legally protected.

“Plain English” isn’t a slogan. It’s how we work.

When we review your contract, you get a clear understanding of what it means and what needs to change. When we draft a contract for you, we keep it as straightforward as possible balancing your commercial needs with protecting your legal position. When you ask us for advice, we tell you what we would actually do in your position. And when something genuinely needs a lawyer's caution, we'll say that too and help you make the right call for your business. Legal advice should bring you more clarity and confidence, not make you more confused. That's the standard we hold ourselves to on every matter and for every client.
