Plain-English, SMB-friendly legal support

Legal advice built for how small business actually works.

mySMB Legal Pty Ltd is an incorporated legal practice designed specifically for small and medium businesses in Australia. We offer practical, commercial, business-aligned legal support in plain English, delivered via monthly subscriptions or fixed-fee packages.

Our mission is simple: to embed practical legal support into your team on an ongoing basis, so you can focus on growth with confidence that your business is legally protected.

“Plain English” isn’t a slogan. It’s how we work. When we review your contract, you get a clear understanding of what it means and what needs to change. When we draft a contract for you, we keep it as straightforward as possible, balancing your commercial needs with protecting your legal position. When you ask us for advice, we tell you what we would actually do in your position. And when something genuinely needs a lawyer’s caution, we’ll say that too and help you make the right call.

mySMB Legal is led by Rebekah Edwards. With more than 25 years in commercial, corporate and financial services law, Rebekah has worked in Sydney and London with top-tier firms Mallesons and Clifford Chance, and investment banks Goldman Sachs and JPMorgan.
